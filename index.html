The Engagement

How This Analysis Was Built

A step-by-step walkthrough of a typical SE discovery-to-proposal process, using real analytical methods and purpose-built calculators to accelerate the work.

1

Customer Discovery & Data Request

Engaged a mid-size e-commerce fulfillment center running two product categories (Health & Beauty, Apparel) across five pick zones. Requested outbound transactions, inbound receipts, inventory snapshot, and SKU master — the minimum viable dataset for a meaningful robotics ROI conversation.

60-day OB history 180-day IB history Full inventory snapshot 5,000-SKU item master
2

Facility Baseline & Slotting Health

Analyzed 1M+ OB pick transactions to establish throughput, zone distribution, and capacity utilization. Discovered the facility was running at 112% capacity — overtime was already required before factoring in any growth. Slotting was reviewed and found to not be optimal, with 1,618 SKUs (32%) identified as candidates for repositioning to better zones.

75 UPH baseline 112% capacity 32% SKUs sub-optimal 6 mi walk/shift
3

Pre-Deployment Operations Analysis

Before sizing any robot fleet, validated that the customer's current operations weren't already leaving efficiency on the table. Slotting health is one of several checks an SE should run — if the fastest SKUs are in the wrong zones, any robot zone assignment built on top of that is wrong by definition. This calculator surfaces that dimension using the customer's own pick data and quantifies the walk distance waste before robots ever enter the picture.

ABC velocity tiering Zone drift detection Walk distance impact
4

Robot Applicability Calculator

Scored every SKU for fit with each Locus robot type against defined criteria. Origin (P2G cobot) works best with high-velocity zone-clusterable SKUs where human travel is the bottleneck. Array + NeuraGrasp works best with uniform robotically-graspable items on extended schedules. H&B (72% of pick volume) scored strongly for Array; Apparel scored for Origin + human collaboration.

Criteria-based scoring Array / NeuraGrasp fit Category routing decision
5

Phased Deployment & ROI Model

Structured a two-phase proposal: Phase 1 deploys 4 Origin robots in Apparel aisles for $360K net annual benefit with zero CapEx. Phase 2 adds 4 Array robots to H&B aisles for autonomous picking and overnight inbound putaway, lifting combined net benefit to $1.4M/yr. Worst-case CapEx pays back in under 4 months.

Phase 1: Origin $360K/yr Phase 2: +Array $1.4M/yr Less than 4 month payback
6

Customer Presentation

Delivered a condensed visual summary to the customer covering current state pain points, the phased robot deployment plan, labor impact, and ROI — structured for a non-technical decision-maker audience rather than as a detailed analysis document.

Current state vs. future state Visual ROI summary Phased roadmap
