The Engagement

How This Analysis Was Built

A step-by-step walkthrough of a typical SE discovery-to-proposal process, using real analytical methods and purpose-built calculators to accelerate the work.

1

Customer Discovery & Data Request

Engaged a mid-size e-commerce fulfillment center running two product categories (Health & Beauty, Apparel) across five pick zones. Requested outbound transactions, inbound receipts, inventory snapshot, and SKU master — the minimum viable dataset for a meaningful robotics ROI conversation.

60-day OB history 180-day IB history Full inventory snapshot 5,000-SKU item master
2

Facility Baseline & Slotting Health

Analyzed 1M+ OB pick transactions to establish throughput, zone distribution, and capacity utilization. Discovered the facility was running at 112% capacity — overtime was already required before factoring in any growth. Identified 1,618 SKUs (32%) with slotting drift from their optimal ABC zone.

75 UPH baseline 112% capacity 32% SKUs misslotted 6 mi walk/shift
3

SKU Slotting Calculator

Built a slotting optimizer that classifies every SKU by velocity tier (ABC), flags mismatches between current zone and optimal zone, and quantifies walk distance waste. This becomes the pre-work recommendation and the foundation for robot zone assignment — you cannot deploy robots well into a broken slot structure.

ABC velocity tiering Zone drift detection Walk distance impact
4

Robot Applicability Calculator

Scored every SKU for fit with each Locus robot type. Origin (P2G cobot) works best with high-velocity zone-clusterable SKUs where human travel is the bottleneck. Array + NeuraGrasp works best with uniform robotically-graspable items on extended schedules. H&B (72% of pick volume) scored strongly for Array; Apparel scored for Origin + human collaboration.

Origin fit score Array / NeuraGrasp fit Category-level routing
5

Phased Deployment & ROI Model

Structured a two-phase proposal: Phase 1 deploys 4 Origin robots in Apparel aisles for $360K net annual benefit with zero CapEx. Phase 2 adds 4 Array robots to H&B aisles for autonomous picking and overnight inbound putaway, lifting combined net benefit to $1.4M/yr. Worst-case CapEx pays back in under 4 months.

Phase 1: Origin $360K/yr Phase 2: +Array $1.4M/yr Less than 4 month payback
6

Customer Presentation

Delivered the full analysis as a structured report covering facility baseline, slotting health, walk distance model, labor cost baseline, Origin and Array deployment plans (including racking scenarios and IB operations), ROI model with flagged assumptions, and a two-phase recommendation roadmap.

9-section report Facility diagrams Phased roadmap

Why calculators? The slotting and applicability analysis are the most time-consuming parts of a typical SE engagement. Building reusable calculators seeded with customer data compresses a 2-3 day analysis into hours and makes the logic transparent to the customer — they can see exactly how the robot recommendations were derived.
