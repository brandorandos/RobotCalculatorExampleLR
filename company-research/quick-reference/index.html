Buyer & User Personas

Who you're selling to vs. who uses the product — with SE angles for each.

Buyer Personas
People Who Own the Purchasing Decision
🏭
The Ops Champion
VP / Director of Operations
Title / Role

VP Operations, Director of Supply Chain, SVP Fulfillment

Company Context

Mid-to-large 3PL or enterprise retailer. Owns DC P&L. Reports to COO or CEO. Accountable for throughput, cost-per-pick, and SLA compliance.

Primary Focus

Throughput, labor efficiency, cost-per-unit, peak season readiness, reducing dependency on hard-to-find labor.

Key Questions

How much will this reduce my cost-per-pick? Can I scale for peak without hiring? What do other 3PLs see for ROI? How long until payback?

Budget / Authority

Often the budget owner OR strong influencer. In larger orgs, needs CFO sign-off for multi-year RaaS commitment.

Pain Points

Labor scarcity and rising wages. High turnover. Missed SLAs during peak. Pressure to do more with less.

Win Signals

Has evaluated AMRs before. Has a live labor problem. Under pressure from CEO/Board to modernize. Has a peer who deployed Locus.

SE Angle

Lead with operational outcomes: UPH gains, cost-per-pick reduction, peak elasticity. Have DHL, GEODIS, and Staples Canada case studies ready.

💰
The Budget Gatekeeper
CFO / VP Finance
Title / Role

CFO, VP Finance, Controller, SVP Finance

Company Context

Reports to CEO. Owns financial approvals for OpEx and CapEx. Risk-averse by nature. Measures success in IRR, payback period, and cost avoidance.

Primary Focus

Total cost of ownership, financial risk management, budget predictability, justifying spend to the board.

Key Questions

What is total cost over 3 years? Is this OpEx or CapEx? What happens if volume drops — am I locked in? What is the exit clause?

Budget / Authority

Final sign-off in many deals. Can approve or kill even when ops team is fully sold. Know their fiscal calendar.

Pain Points

Unpredictable labor costs. CapEx avoidance after write-off experiences. Subscription fatigue. Fear of long-term commitment to unproven tech.

Win Signals

Has approved SaaS tools before. CFO is asking questions rather than delegating. Finance team modeling scenarios — that's a buy signal.

SE Angle

Lead with RaaS OpEx story: no CapEx outlay, predictable monthly cost. Have a 3-year TCO model ready. Address exit clauses proactively.

🔌
The Integration Gatekeeper
IT Director / VP Technology
Title / Role

Director of IT, VP Technology, CTO (smaller company), Director of Enterprise Systems

Company Context

Owns WMS, ERP, and all technology integrations. Reports to COO or CEO. Manages a lean team stretched across multiple systems.

Primary Focus

Technical feasibility, integration complexity, data security, system uptime, and not creating new tickets for their team.

Key Questions

How does this connect to our WMS? What APIs? Who owns the data? What's the uptime SLA? How do you handle security?

Budget / Authority

Rarely owns budget but can block or delay by raising technical concerns. Get to IT early — ambush creates gatekeeping.

Pain Points

Custom WMS integrations that break. Vendor lock-in. Data security compliance pressure. IT bandwidth — already stretched too thin.

Win Signals

Has done AMR or WMS integrations before. WMS is modern with REST API. IT team is lean (wants low-maintenance). Brings system architect to call.

SE Angle

Lead with pre-built WMS connectors and Fortress Cybersecurity Award (2 consecutive years). Offer API docs during sales — not post-signature.

User Personas
People Who Use the Product Day-to-Day
📋
The Daily Driver
DC Operations Manager / Warehouse Manager
Title / Role

DC Manager, Warehouse Manager, Shift Manager, Operations Manager

Company Context

Manages day-to-day floor operations. Direct reports are supervisors and pickers. Reports to VP Operations.

Primary Focus

Daily throughput targets, labor coordination with robots, robot uptime, shift-by-shift KPI attainment.

Daily Workflows

Reviews LocusHub dashboard each morning. Monitors UPH and robot utilization. Coordinates robot-to-picker assignments. Handles exception management.

Frustrations

Robots going offline during peak. Unclear why throughput dipped. Manual workarounds when system errors occur.

Technical Skills

Moderate. Comfortable with dashboards. Not a developer. Uses LocusHub daily. Escalates technical issues rather than debugging.

Win Signals

Uses LocusHub daily without prompting. Shares performance screenshots with VP Ops. Requests more robots. Becomes your best reference at 90 days.

SE Angle

Demo LocusHub live. Show fleet re-tasking ease. Address support SLA. Include them in deployment planning — creates ownership, not resistance.

📦
The Floor Worker
Warehouse Associate (Picker)
Title / Role

Warehouse Associate, Order Picker, Fulfillment Specialist, DC Associate

Company Context

Front-line worker walking alongside Origin robots during P2G picking. Varies widely in tenure, language, and technical comfort.

Primary Focus

Completing daily pick quota. Physical safety near robots. Keeping up with robot pace. Not losing their job to a machine.

Daily Workflows

Follows pick prompts on Origin robot touchscreen. Places items in tote. Responds to SDL task routing. Moves between pick zones as robot navigates.

Frustrations

Robot pace too fast or inconsistent. Pick instructions that are unclear. Robot navigating to wrong zone.

Technical Skills

Low to moderate. Uses robot touchscreen only. No system-level access. May not speak English as first language — icon-heavy UI matters.

Win Signals

Floor adoption is smooth post-go-live. Low error rates. Associates stop asking supervisors for help within the first week.

SE Angle

Address job security concern in change management plan. 80% training time reduction (DHL). Include floor training plan in your deployment proposal.

⚙️
The System Owner
IT Systems Admin / Integration Manager
Title / Role

Systems Administrator, WMS Admin, Integration Manager, IT Manager

Company Context

Manages the technical bridge between LocusONE and the customer's WMS and ERP. Often a single person covering multiple systems.

Primary Focus

System uptime, API connection health, order sync accuracy, error resolution, data integrity between LocusONE and WMS.

Daily Workflows

Monitors API connection health. Reviews error logs. Resolves order sync issues. Manages Locus software update windows. Escalates L2/L3 issues to Locus support.

Frustrations

Black-box integrations they didn't build. API changes that break connections without warning. Unclear documentation. Slow support during peak season.

Technical Skills

High. Comfortable with REST APIs, JSON, system logs, and network troubleshooting. Will read the docs. Will test the integration themselves.

Win Signals

Validates integration docs proactively. Brings architecture questions to the table. Confirms API connectivity before go-live sign-off.

SE Angle

Offer API documentation during the sales process — not post-signature. Position Locus Deployment Engineers as ongoing partners, not a one-time setup team.
