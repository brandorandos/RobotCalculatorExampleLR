## The Engagement Story

A mid-size e-commerce fulfillment center running at 112% capacity (overtime required today). Requested customer data, ran slotting health analysis, built robot applicability scoring, and produced a two-phase deployment recommendation:

- **Phase 1 — Origin:** $360K net annual benefit, zero CapEx
- **Phase 2 — Array:** $1.4M net annual benefit, worst-case CapEx payback < 4 months

All data is synthetic — statistically realistic but not tied to any real customer.
